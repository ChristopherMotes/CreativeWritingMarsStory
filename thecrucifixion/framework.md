Stevie's father forbids him from finding him again until Stevie's time as the Mask is done. The scene should be tear jerking, mismatching the stoicism of father and son.

We see how women in the Secondshima militia's fill the critical role of nurse and care giver. Gloria isn't entireley healed. We findout she still has nightmare about her previous husband.
