### New Brownsvillle Structure

New Brownsville, was the original dome created on the planet Mars, by a space company "wink, wink". Then the Chinese landed farther away. Then the Russians tried to build a dome and throught better of it. Then USA, EU, and Japan built a new dome next to New Brownsville. Then the third dome Vostoshima. 'Cause plot stuff, all three domes became New Brownsville, Muskashima, Secondshima, and Vostoshima. All the Martians live under a very loose and weak global government. New Brownsville, is now a very weak federal government over Muskashima, Secondshima, and Vostoshima. A new dome is being built to contain all three domes. That will break the federation.

#### Secondshima

Secondshima started as a combined effort between the USA, EU, and Japan. The corrupt beauracrats lost power power quickly. The dome itself is the largest and best built dome, but it's internal structure lacks any advantages of centralized planning. Secondshima's culture runs on a brief bill of rights.

As all govenenment is inherantly evil. The people must have the tools to defend the Republic and to defend their neighbors from the Republic. The individual must have the tools to defend himself from crimals, government, and the tyranny of the majority. The majority must have the tools to defend themselves the the perpetual victimhood of the individual or minority groups.

1. Each male citizen aged 16-65 is a member of the well regulated militia and must be armed to protect and enforce the preamble
2. No action taken in a citizens home, including sight, sound, and smell, maybe investigated, crimialized, prohibited, or infringed upon in anyway by the govenrment or neighbors unless the action represent a threat of death or greivous bodily harm to neighbors.
3. Each citizen must serve jury duty annually to  support the fair and free flow of courts.
4. No citizen shall be held in jail upon arrest for more than 34 hours, without grand jury indictment or finding from a bail jury.
