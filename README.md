
# New Brownville Mars

This is some creative writing that I'm doing. Mostly it's about working with AI. Mostly, it's Claude Haiku, 'cause that's what my IDE plugged to. Also, grok. I really like grok. Within the first few exercises, I'll admit I'm stuggling to let AI do the heavy lifting when I should. I know I should operate more as Product Owner and tester. Then let AI carry the story.

## Read order

Start by reading Intro.md. Then, in any order SacarletMask.md, TheWhisper.md, and DeepBlue.md. Story Lines will grow in subdirectories if I ever get to them.

## Vigelantes

### ScarletMask

Scarlet Mask has a lot of who I thought I was as younger man. He's really personal. I write most of the dialog and build the world. Secondshima is a double entandre. That dome has a lot of my personal beliefs about liberty and the American Second Amendment.

### The Whisper

The Whisper is a hybrid of GI Joe's Snake Eyes, Batman, and Zorro. I'm not sure if the Whisper is a robot/drone operated by Leo Musk or Musk in a super suit. It might play out as the story goes or I'll leave it as a mystery. Leo is taken from Leo Wong in Futurama. Musk of course from Elon Musk. I write a lot of Jimmy Nuts' dialog. He's Jimmy McNaulty from the Wire on Mars. The world of Muskashima is my best attempt to create a world of efficient government order, that I wouldn't lose my mind if I lived in. It's very authoritarian, but with a real focus on human decenecy, not the accumulation of power by a few.

### Deep Blue

Originally Deep Blue was gonna be another character kinda based off of Cobra Commander. But it struck me as awesome and poetic, to make Deep Blue an AI mostly written by whatever AI is plugged to my IDE do the heavy lifting. I provide very little guidance. Just some stuff to hold the world together. Vosteshima has very little guidance from me. Here's the only thing I passed to an IA about Vostoshima
