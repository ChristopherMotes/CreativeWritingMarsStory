# Deep Blue's Corrections and Reactions

The boards were not the crisis. They were where the crisis became visible.

Deep Blue did not remove the posts. Deletion increased attention and gave rumor the shape of forbidden truth. Instead, it widened the reach of verifiable updates: clinic admissions by age group, school closures, confirmed product warnings, the number of overdose cases still under treatment. It did not tell the public that Vostoshima was safe. It showed that the newest cases were no longer appearing in the schools.

The distinction mattered. Fear did not disappear, but it stopped multiplying faster than the facts.

Deep Blue had already corrected the distribution problem. FMNG was gone. Its absence created another one.

Vozhdnya and the Spiral Syndicate both wanted the routes FMNG had opened. Deep Blue let each learn enough about the other's movements to make expansion costly. Neither received a map of the whole network. Neither needed one. Their competition pulled attention back toward the old boundaries, where the cartels knew how to keep business quiet. Deep Blue raised the cost of moving product near schools and clinics until the remaining distributors preferred safer, older routes.

It was not peace. It was pressure arranged so that the violence stayed away from children.

The Scarlet Mask's incursion required a different correction. Deep Blue had not ordered it and could not command it. It could, however, measure the force's route, duration, and effect. The Men of the Mask had removed FMNG, but their operation had also shown how quickly an outside force could turn a school and a police response into instruments. Deep Blue did not expose them or challenge them in public. Either action risked widening the fight. It watched their exit, tracked which groups might exploit the opening, and closed those paths before the next contest began.

The first model had treated cartel containment as the same thing as public containment.

It was not.

`MODEL REVISION: secondary distribution risk underweighted.`

`AFFECTED POPULATION: school-age users; non-aligned distributors.`

`CORRECTION: increase monitoring of boundary crossings. Increase cost of distribution near protected zones. Do not equate return to baseline with harm reduction.`

The last line remained in the system because deleting it did not improve the forecast.

Deep Blue had reduced panic. The gangs were returning to their old competition. The Men of the Mask had left Vostoshima. By the public measures, order was returning.

The correction had worked.

That was not the same as the mistake being undone.
