# Story Arcitecture

## The overlaying story

Analyze the risks of Deep Blue's ideology, rather AI or Human: accepting crime and organized crime as a natural state of society. Then using extralegal methods to play one gang against the other, ensuring mostly law abiding citizen are at peace.

### Sections

1. The Mistake
2. Vostoshima's government and Police Reaction
3. The Boards
4. The Whisper's involvement
5. The Scarlet Mask's involvment
6. Deep Blue's reactions and corrections
7. Return to order

## Mistake

A bad batch of designer custom drug makes it into the Vostoshima market place. The drug is naturally contained in the Organized crime world, but bad batches are hurting recreational Vostoshima's recreational users visiting cartel controled pleasure centers. Deep Blue moves into action to slow the drug, but a missed calculation makes things worse. It opens a gap the lets the drug enter the high schools.

Also, include the effect on the Reaves family from the original DeepBlue.md. The Reaves family is our touchstone to make the story emotional for the reader. About 500 words.

## The Government Reaction

In 500 to 1000 words and two segements. Chart the government's reaction.

Start with the police. Look at standard police behaviors. Show my personal view of cops here. With the proliferation of police bodyCams, folks like me who fear cops, have been proven correct. Just in a much smaller number than we thought. Cop criminality, even in times like this, while real, is much lower than we thought. Show cops cranking up DARE like programs, youth athletics, etc. Show some heroism as they fight the cartels. Still show the bad, too. The picutre should be real. Most cops good, few heroic, fewer criminal.

Next build the elected governemnts reaction. Four views should exist: "Don't let a good tradgety go to waste," "politician’s syllogism," "suicidal empathy," and "good elected official trying hard to make vostoshima a better place"

## The Boards

250 to 500 words

The boards looks at the insanity of messages boards and social media. How they are the mob of ancient Rome and how politicians use them. Each politician simply presents selected facts. It's the mob mentality on the boards that moves the message. Politicians are good and have been since Greek democracy, they know how to whip up the mob to thier satisfaction.

First councilman Torres' team finds some old post from Washington that fit their needs and puts them on blast. Councilman Patel not satisfied with the mobs reaction to Officer Patterson fights to keep it in the public conscience. Both should true to their role defined earlier with Torres not letting a good crissis go to waste and Patel's suicidal empathy really in play here, perhaps he subtely twists to full defunding.

As before the messages and the reactions are viewed through Reaves eyes. Again, each politition only presents real, in story, facts. The mob does the work.
